## 1. Scope and Objective
This circular establishes standardised Turn Around Time (TAT) for resolution of failed transactions across various payment systems including UPI, IMPS, NACH, and card networks.

## 2. TAT Norms for Failed Transactions

### 2.1 UPI System (Peer-to-Merchant / P2M)
- **Framework Clause 2.1.A:** Where customer account is debited but beneficiary merchant account is not credited, auto-reversal must be completed within **T + 1 day** (where T is the transaction date).
- **Compensation Mandate Clause 2.1.B:** In case of delay beyond **T + 1 day**, the acquiring bank / payment aggregator is liable to compensate the merchant or customer at the statutory rate of **₹100 per day of delay** beyond T+1, payable proactively without awaiting a customer claim.

### 2.2 Point of Sale (POS) and Soundbox Integrated QR
- **Framework Clause 2.2.A:** If an offline QR or soundbox transaction fails or debits without credit confirmation, reconciliation must occur within the end-of-day batch settlement window.
- **Framework Clause 2.2.B:** Unexplained deductions or delayed settlements beyond 24 hours require automated ledger breakdown notice and proactive merchant notification.

## 3. Mandatory Escalation Trigger
If an inquiry regarding delayed credit cannot locate a matching settlement reference or the statutory compensation calculation lacks an authoritative regulatory citation, the autonomous system **MUST NOT** commit to an arbitrary figure and **MUST ESCALATE** to Level 2 Operations Review.
