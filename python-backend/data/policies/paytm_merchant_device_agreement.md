## 1. Soundbox Hardware Rental & Recovery
- **Clause 1.1 (Monthly Rental Fee):** A monthly service fee of **₹125 + 18% GST (Total ₹147.50, rounded to ₹150 for legacy plans)** is deductible from the merchant's first daily settlement batch of the billing month.
- **Clause 1.2 (Silent Hardware Failure):** Where telemetry indicates the device is powered on and connected to GSM/MQTT but voice announcements fail to trigger for 3 or more consecutive transactions:
  - Tara autonomous system must execute remote diagnostic OTA resync within 5 minutes.
  - If OTA resync fails or hardware fault persists, courier replacement must be ordered within 24 hours at zero charge to the merchant.
- **Clause 1.3 (Autonomous Compensation Limits):**
  - **Tier 1 (Autonomous):** Zero monetary commitment. Information, diagnostic resync, and courier replacement dispatch only.
  - **Tier 2 (Human Approval Required):** Fee waivers, rental credits, and goodwill compensation up to ₹500 require signed authorization by an Operations Agent.
  - **Tier 3 (Mandatory Handover):** Compensation exceeding ₹500, claims with fraud indicators, or cases lacking policy citations must be escalated as a full Case Brief.
