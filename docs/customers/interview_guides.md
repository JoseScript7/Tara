# Tara — Interview Guides

> **Instructions:** Use these questions in semi-structured interviews. Record responses with consent. Never collect real account numbers, balances, or personal financial details. Anonymize all quotes.

---

## Guide 1: Merchants (Kirana, Café, Pharmacy/Clinic Owners)

**Target:** 8–12 conversations across personas 1–3

### Opening
- Thank you for your time. We're building a tool to help merchants like you when payment issues happen. I'd like to understand your experience — there are no right or wrong answers.

### Questions

1. **Tell me about the last time your soundbox or payment didn't work.** What happened, step by step?
   - _Follow-up:_ How did you first notice? What were you doing at the time?

2. **What did you do first?** Who did you call or message? How long until it was fixed?
   - _Follow-up:_ Did you try anything yourself before calling? What did support tell you to do?

3. **What did that cost you** (lost sales, time, stress)?
   - _Follow-up:_ Did customers leave? Did you lose trust with any regular customers?

4. **How do you check whether you've been paid?** When did the numbers last look wrong?
   - _Follow-up:_ Do you check the app, a message, the soundbox, or a bank statement? How often?

5. **Do you prefer to type, speak, send a photo, or call?** In which language?
   - _Follow-up:_ If an assistant spoke to you in [their language], would that help?

6. **Have you noticed fee or settlement changes?** How did you find out?
   - _Follow-up:_ Were the changes explained clearly? Did you understand why the amount changed?

7. **What would make you trust an automated assistant?** What would make you stop using it?
   - _Follow-up:_ Would you trust it more if it showed you proof (like a screenshot or receipt)?

8. **If an assistant could fix it but needed your OK, how would you like to give it?**
   - _Follow-up:_ A button in the app? A voice confirmation? An OTP?

### Closing
- Is there anything else about your payment experience that frustrates you that we haven't talked about?
- Would you be willing to try a prototype and give us feedback later?

## Guide 2: Ops / Support Agents

**Target:** 3–5 conversations

### Questions

1. **What are the top five repeated ticket types** you see every day?
   - _Follow-up:_ Which ones take the most time? Which could be automated?

2. **What information do you re-ask the merchant for** that should already be in the system?
   - _Follow-up:_ How much time does this re-asking waste?

3. **What do you never trust a machine to decide?** Why?
   - _Follow-up:_ Has an automated system ever made a mistake you had to fix?

4. **What does a good escalation note contain?**
   - _Follow-up:_ What's usually missing when a case is escalated to you?

5. **If an AI assistant handled the initial diagnosis and proposed a solution, what would you need to see before approving it?**
   - _Follow-up:_ Would you trust it more if it showed its reasoning and evidence?

6. **What makes a particularly frustrating day?**
   - _Follow-up:_ What tools or information would make your job easier?

## Guide 3: Field Technicians

**Target:** 2–3 conversations

### Questions

1. **What device problems do you see most often?**
   - _Follow-up:_ Are there common causes (water damage, power issues, firmware)?

2. **What do you wish you knew before arriving at a merchant's location?**
   - _Follow-up:_ Do you get enough context about the issue? The device history?

3. **How do you confirm a fix is working?**
   - _Follow-up:_ Do you run a test transaction? Check telemetry? Get merchant sign-off?

4. **Have you ever been sent to fix something that turned out to be a different problem?**
   - _Follow-up:_ How often does this happen? What would prevent it?

5. **How do you communicate with the merchant?**
   - _Follow-up:_ Language barriers? Do they understand the technical explanation?

## Guide 4: Compliance / Risk Reviewer

**Target:** 1–2 conversations (if reachable)

### Questions

1. **What must be logged** for every merchant interaction and action?
   - _Follow-up:_ Are there specific RBI/NPCI requirements you're thinking of?

2. **Which actions always need human approval**, no matter how clear-cut they seem?
   - _Follow-up:_ Is there a threshold (amount, type, risk level)?

3. **What does a good audit trail look like** for a dispute or compensation decision?
   - _Follow-up:_ What's the minimum evidence you'd accept?

4. **What are the biggest compliance risks** with an AI making operational decisions?
   - _Follow-up:_ How would you want to be able to review and override AI decisions?

5. **Are there specific circular or regulation references** that should be cited in every financial decision?

## Interview Logistics

| Item | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Consent** | Verbal consent recorded at the start. Explain: research for a college project, no real data collected. |
| **Duration** | 15–20 minutes per interview |
| **Recording** | Audio with consent, or detailed written notes during the interview |
| **Data** | Never collect real account numbers, balances, Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details |
| **Output** | Anonymized quotes and themes → `synthesis_template.md` |
| **If unreachable** | Document as "proxy research" using app store reviews, support forums, news articles. Label clearly. |
