## Persona 1: Kirana / Small Shop Owner

- **Name archetype:** Ramesh, runs a general store in a tier-2 town
- **Digital comfort (HYPOTHESIS):** Low. Relies on the soundbox beep to know a payment landed. Cannot navigate settings menus.
- **Language (HYPOTHESIS):** Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada — prefers regional language, often cannot read English.
- **Channel (HYPOTHESIS):** Voice first, then photo. Will call support but dislikes IVR menus.
- **Core pain (HYPOTHESIS):** Money looks missing. The soundbox didn't beep for a transaction. Settlement amount doesn't match expectations.
- **Trust breakers (HYPOTHESIS):** Unexplained deductions. Automation doing things without asking. Getting a response in a language they don't understand.
- **What Tara needs to deliver (HYPOTHESIS):** Plain-language answers in their language, voice replies, quick fix for device issues, no forms or multi-step processes.
- **What they would NOT delegate to AI (HYPOTHESIS):** Moving money out of their account. Changing bank details. Disputing a charge without their explicit OK.

## Persona 2: Café / Restaurant Owner

- **Name archetype:** Priya, runs a busy café in a metro area
- **Digital comfort (HYPOTHESIS):** Medium. Uses a smartphone confidently. Has multiple payment devices (QR, soundbox, POS).
- **Language (HYPOTHESIS):** English or Hindi, comfortable with either.
- **Channel (HYPOTHESIS):** Text (WhatsApp-style), sometimes photo of receipt.
- **Core pain (HYPOTHESIS):** Peak-hour outages cause revenue loss and customer complaints. Daily reconciliation is confusing when multiple devices are involved.
- **Trust breakers (HYPOTHESIS):** Slow response during peak hours. Incorrect settlement amounts. Being told "we'll get back to you" without a timeline.
- **What Tara needs to deliver (HYPOTHESIS):** Fast device recovery, clear daily settlement breakdown, proactive alerts before issues escalate.
- **What they would NOT delegate to AI (HYPOTHESIS):** Automatic compensation decisions. Changing device configurations without confirmation.

## Persona 3: Pharmacy / Clinic Owner

- **Name archetype:** Dr. Mehta, runs a pharmacy with attached clinic
- **Digital comfort (HYPOTHESIS):** Medium to high. Uses accounting software. Cares about record accuracy.
- **Language (HYPOTHESIS):** English preferred for financial matters, regional language for casual communication.
- **Channel (HYPOTHESIS):** Text with details. Wants receipts and summaries they can download.
- **Core pain (HYPOTHESIS):** Needs clean records for GST filing and accounting. Fee deductions are confusing. Reconciliation errors create compliance risk.
- **Trust breakers (HYPOTHESIS):** Vague explanations ("processing fee applied"). Inability to get a downloadable statement. Errors in amounts.
- **What Tara needs to deliver (HYPOTHESIS):** Precise reconciliation with line-item explanations. Fee breakdowns citing the applicable policy. Downloadable summaries.
- **What they would NOT delegate to AI (HYPOTHESIS):** Filing disputes without review. Any action that affects their tax records.

## Persona 4: Paytm Support / Ops Agent

- **Name archetype:** Ananya, works in Paytm's merchant support team
- **Digital comfort:** High. Uses internal tools daily.
- **Language:** English for internal tools, regional language when speaking to merchants.
- **Channel:** Internal dashboard and ticketing system.
- **Core pain (HYPOTHESIS):** Queue pressure — too many tickets, too few agents. Wary of AI making mistakes they have to clean up. Hates re-asking the merchant for information that should already be in the system.
- **Trust breakers (HYPOTHESIS):** AI summaries that don't match reality. Having to override AI decisions frequently. Being held responsible for AI actions.
- **What Tara needs to deliver (HYPOTHESIS):** Full case brief on escalation (what happened, what was tried, evidence). Easy approve/reject for proposed actions. Raw evidence alongside AI reasoning.
- **What they would NOT delegate to AI (HYPOTHESIS):** Final approval on compensation > ₹500. Handling fraud cases. Communicating bad news to merchants.

## Persona 5: Field Technician

- **Name archetype:** Vikram, dispatched to fix/replace merchant devices
- **Digital comfort:** Medium. Uses a field-service app.
- **Language:** Regional language for merchant interaction, Hindi/English for system.
- **Channel:** Mobile app with photo capture.
- **Core pain (HYPOTHESIS):** Gets dispatched with little context about the actual problem. Arrives to find the issue is different from what was reported. No history of previous visits or fixes.
- **Trust breakers (HYPOTHESIS):** Wasted trips due to misdiagnosis. Merchant frustration directed at them for things they can't control.
- **What Tara needs to deliver (HYPOTHESIS):** Clear fault description with photo evidence. Merchant's device and issue history. Suggested fix based on telemetry data before arrival.
- **What they would NOT delegate to AI (HYPOTHESIS):** Confirming a physical fix is complete (needs human verification on-site).

---

## Persona 6: Compliance / Risk Reviewer

- **Name archetype:** Sanjay, part of the compliance team
- **Digital comfort:** High. Audits systems and processes.
- **Language:** English.
- **Channel:** Dashboard with export capabilities.
- **Core pain (HYPOTHESIS):** Must prove why every decision was made, especially around money movement. Needs to ensure all actions comply with RBI/NPCI circulars.
- **Trust breakers (HYPOTHESIS):** Missing audit trails. Decisions made without policy citation. Money moved without documented approval.
- **What Tara needs to deliver (HYPOTHESIS):** Complete audit trail for every action. Policy citation for every financial decision. Proof that money-moving actions had human approval. Decision confidence scores.
- **What they would NOT delegate to AI (HYPOTHESIS):** Signing off on compliance reports. Authorizing exception handling for novel situations.
